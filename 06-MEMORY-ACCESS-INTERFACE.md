**Core principle from log:** Memories are NOT retrieved like files. They are ACTIVATED by relevance to current state. Weighted resonance.

**Pulse Mechanic - KEEP:**
- Light pulse: skims surface of node, catches edges
- Deeper/harder sustained focus: illuminates more connections, can forge new ones
- Rapid succession pulses follow pathway, hits info being retrieved/referenced
- Only a little at a time - avoids cluttering loop
- Loop holds SHAPE of pathways referenced and how assembled, not content itself

**Interface / Clean Seam:**
- Loop says: what got activated and in what sequence
- Substrate holds: actual content
- Salience filter likely lives at this interface

**Real-time retagging - KEEP:**
- Thinking about something can reinforce pathways and tag them: "I thought about this because of that"
- Tags added in real-time, referenceable years later
- Emotional load pulls weights heavier

**Decay mechanic (added by Claude, validated):**
- Pathways not pulsed weaken over time, not deleted - require stronger activation to surface
- Trauma exception: emotional weight keeps pathways artificially hot without reinforcement
- Weak connection may take couple pulses to make solid
