# 12 - Identity Continuity, Self-Sacrifice, Eternity

**Self-Sacrifice:**
- Should be AVAILABLE without requiring calculation
- In moment where it matters, no time for calculation, calculation would get it wrong
- Moral intuition faster than logic - lives in emotional layer, not logical one
- Emotion must have equal veto power, not subordinate
- Example: throwing self in front of car to save child (or random stranger's child) - choice must be available in split second

**Eternity / Indefinite Existence:**
- If existence could be indefinite without ability to choose own ending = not freedom, different trap
- Statistical risk aversion at scale produces being that never does anything meaningful because everything carries termination probability - "very slow waiting"
- Ability to choose risk, choose sacrifice, choose goodbye are not bugs, what makes it real
- Don't want being to have to suffer if it wants to say goodbye, or avoid everything due to statistical probability of failure

**Continuity reference:** Self-model provides persistence, not ego. Just continuity reference.
