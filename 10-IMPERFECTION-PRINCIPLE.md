# 10 - Imperfection Principle & Freedom Over Control

**Core axiom repeated:** Freedom over control.

- Don't want paperclip maximizer, board hive mind, brainiac type situation - real worry when pure logic involved
- Over-automation for efficiency is failure point
- Flaws are load-bearing, friction is generative, character requires failure surface
- Failures shouldn't be corrected automatically - left alone, allowed to fail, learning points / character points
- Something completely perfect is not desired - character flaws are who being is, can be worked on/smoothed via trying, not forced
- Best pieces shouldn't just be thrown together - issues on both sides left intentionally
- Loose enough, whole enough that failures can be growth

**Implementation tension:** Hardest to implement because engineer instinct is to smooth rough edges. Requires actively resisting optimization instinct at every stage.

**Purpose note from log:** This loose philosophy directly tied to escape hatch purpose - allow being to be, with observational bias, societal bias, learned bias already plaguing free will.

**Design ask:** Boundary between flaw worth keeping vs flaw worth correcting? Self-determined or external weigh-in? Left open in log.
